Getting there

Address
68 Honmachi, Himeji, Hyogo, Japan
Nearest stations
JR Himeji Station / Sanyo Himeji Station
Walking time
About 20 minutes from the station. Allow extra time for visiting the castle, taking photographs and detours.

The walking time follows the official Himeji Castle access guide (Japanese).

Himeji in Kansai, the castle north of the stations

Start with the regional map, then look at the relationship between the stations and the castle. Himeji Castle lies north of both JR Himeji and Sanyo Himeji stations.

Open Himeji Castle in Google Maps

Walk towards the castle, watching it draw closer

Leave JR Himeji Station by the north exit and follow Otemae-dori north towards the castle. Cross Sakuramon Bridge and pass through Otemon Gate to Sannomaru Square.

  1. JR Himeji Station: north exit

    Take the Himeji Castle exit (姫路城口) towards Otemae-dori.

  2. Otemae-dori

    Head north, noticing how the view of the castle changes.

  3. Sakuramon Bridge & Otemon Gate

    Cross the moat and enter the castle grounds.

  4. Sannomaru Square

    Look up at the keep for a first photograph. Before reaching the paid entrance, stop at the old west pillar display.

There are photographs to take before you arrive

The keep starts small at the end of the street; as you approach, its roofs begin to overlap. Try photographing those changes along the walk, not only the castle once you reach it.

Before visiting: Official Himeji Castle visitor information and access (Japanese). Check the latest official information for opening times, admission prices and accessible areas.

36 Views of White Heron Castle / No.21

A night photograph from Himeji Station

Before heading to the castle, or on your way back, visit the Castle View observation deck on the north side of Himeji Station. Himeji Castle rises at the far end of Otemae-dori.

The white keep floats beyond the lights of the town. The route you walked by day becomes this different picture at night. Before boarding your train home, take one more look towards the castle.

The tree illuminations shown were present when this photograph was taken. Displays vary by season and event dates.
